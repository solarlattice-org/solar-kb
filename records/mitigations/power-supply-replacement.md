This action swaps out the power supply board in a Yaskawa Solectria XGI 1500 inverter, following the maker's customer-facing instructions. The spare part is one UTC001400 auxiliary power board. First connect the black and red wires to the screw terminals with a #1 Phillips bit and a torque driver, tightening each to 10.2 in-lbs. Take care not to damage the sub boards that stand at right angles nearby. Line the new board up on its mounting pegs and press it upward until the tabs click. You may need to pull the tabs aside to get the holes aligned. Plug the power supply harness back onto the board pins. The connector only fits one way. The procedure ends with a check that the inverter is working properly.
