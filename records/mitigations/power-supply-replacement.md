Power supply replacement means removing a faulty internal power supply board from an inverter and fitting a new one, to correct an internal power supply fault. Yaskawa Solectria publishes customer instructions for replacing this board in the XGI 1500 inverter, and the replacement part is listed as UTC001400. To install it, connect the black and red wires to the screw terminals with a Phillips bit and a torque driver, taking care not to damage the small sub boards that stand at right angles to the main board. Line the board up on its mounting pegs and push it upward until the mounting tabs click and hold it in place. Plug the power supply harness back onto the board's pins. The connector only fits one way. Finish by refitting the right enclosure cover and tightening its screws to the specified torque. Before you replace anything for a control power supply fault, one manufacturer says to check that the internal and external supply switches are set correctly and that the supply terminals are tight. A simple setting or wiring problem can cause the same fault.
