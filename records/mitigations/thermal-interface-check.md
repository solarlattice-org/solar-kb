This check is done when an inverter assembly is replaced. Before you fit the new assembly, inspect the thermal grease on its rear side, where it sits on the power modules. Lift the new assembly straight up out of its packaging with care so the grease is not damaged or contaminated. Then check the grease pattern for damage and dirt. If the grease pattern is damaged, replace the assembly. Small specks of dirt can be lifted off carefully with a pointed tool, as long as the pattern stays intact. On some assemblies, use a magnifying glass to check that the honeycomb pattern at the edge of the grease has not dried out. Dried grease shows flat, sometimes two-coloured combs. Do not use an assembly whose grease has dried out. Before installation, the grease pattern must be undamaged and free of impurities. After removing the old assembly, wipe off any leftover old grease with a lint-free cloth and a little alcohol, such as isopropanol. When fitting the new assembly, do not smudge or contaminate the squeegee pattern on the power modules. There are two assembly types that are not compatible with each other, so always fit the same type that was already installed. For full repair and safety instructions, see the service manual.
