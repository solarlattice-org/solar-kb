Replacing the AC relay assembly is a service procedure for SMA inverters, including Sunny Tripower and Sunny Island models. It is covered by dedicated replacement manuals and spare part sets. Before you start, follow the safety instructions. On the STP60 family, check the PSU board hardware version first, because an older PSU board must be replaced with a newer one before the AC relay module can be swapped. To remove the old assembly, take off the mounting plates and mark where each power cable connects and which way its lug faces. Then disconnect the flat ribbon cable, the four-pin cable and the power cables, and unscrew the assembly while squeezing the locking tabs on the circuit board holders. Touch the assembly only at its marked touchable areas. Move the air duct from the old assembly onto the new one. Insert the new assembly and check that the board holders snap in. Reconnect all cables in their original positions, making sure the ribbon cable latches click into place and the cable lugs and insulation sit properly aligned. Under the SMA warranty, a claimant trained by SMA may be sent a single assembly instead of a complete replacement device. The defective assembly must then be returned with a valid RMA number.
