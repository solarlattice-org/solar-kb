name: Bypass diode replacement
---
Bypass diode replacement means swapping out a failed bypass diode in the module junction box. The sources say this is technically feasible. It is a corrective action for Bypass Diode Failure. Diodes usually fail in one of two ways: open circuit or short circuit. Before replacing a diode, confirm the fault by testing it directly with a multimeter in diode or ohmmeter mode. This is the best way to tell whether it is shorted or open. The recommended repair is to replace the affected modules or qualified diode assemblies, fix any upstream mismatch or shading, and then check string behavior after the repair. In practice, operators usually replace the whole module rather than just the diode. Modules with a failed diode that are not replaced should be closely monitored. Open-circuit diodes are hard to detect and normally go unnoticed unless the module has significant mismatch, so some failed diodes may never be found and replaced. Avoiding partial shading extends bypass diode lifetime and lowers the chance of further failures.
