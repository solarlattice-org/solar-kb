Replacing the IGBT matrix is a corrective inverter repair. An inverter specialist does it when needed, and it falls under the inverter warranty. Yaskawa publishes customer-facing instructions for replacing the IGBT assembly in the XGI 1500 inverter. You reach the IGBT assemblies through the service panel and take each one out by removing its mounting screws. Installation takes two technicians. One holds the new assembly so the heatsink holes line up with the Drop-In panel. The other fastens it from the front with the original hardware, using a T25 Torx driver torqued to the specified value. Next, fasten the AC bus bars and the red inductor wires, then fit the snubber caps to the IGBT terminals and torque the screws. Finally, reconnect the ribbon cable to the Gate Adaptor board. Check the connector orientation and make sure the latches are closed.
