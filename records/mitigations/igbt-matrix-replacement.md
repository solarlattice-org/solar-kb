- IGBT Assembly Replacement

IGBT matrix replacement means swapping out the IGBT power switching assembly inside an inverter. It is a corrective repair for a power stage fault. O&M practice treats it as an as-needed inverter repair carried out by an inverter specialist, normally covered under the inverter warranty. Inverter fault codes such as power stage error, power stage trip and IGBT temperature can point to a problem in the power stage. Removing and installing the assembly takes two people, because the heat sink can fall off the back of the inverter. Take care not to lose the O-rings. After the new assembly is fastened to the drop-in panel, refit the AC bus bars, inductor wires and snubber capacitors to the specified torques. Then reconnect the gate adaptor ribbon cable with the latches closed.
