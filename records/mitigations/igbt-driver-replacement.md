id: mit:igbt-driver-replacement

name: IGBT driver board replacement
---
Yaskawa publishes a customer-facing procedure for replacing the IGBT assembly on the XGI 1500 inverter. The gate driver interface on this assembly is the Gate Adaptor board. The same manual set also has a procedure for putting the XGI 1500 into an electrically safe state, along with torque specifications and spare-part lists. Two technicians fit the new assembly. One holds it so the heatsink holes line up with the Drop-In panel at the back of the inverter, and the other fastens it from the front with the hardware removed earlier, using a T25 Torx driver. Next, the AC bus bars are fastened to the IGBTs, and the red inductor wires and bus bars are fixed to the isolating standoffs. Then the two snubber capacitors are mounted on the IGBT terminals. The ribbon cable is plugged into the Gate Adaptor board. Check the connector orientation and make sure the latches close around the mating connector. Finally, the NTC temperature harness goes onto the two-pin connectors of the Gate Adaptor boards. The shortest branch goes to the Phase A (top) IGBT and the longest branch to the Phase C (bottom) IGBT.
