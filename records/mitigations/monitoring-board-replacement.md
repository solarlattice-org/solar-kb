name: Monitoring board replacement
---
The alarm is called Faulty Monitoring Unit or Flash Fault and is rated minor. It points to a flash memory failure on the monitoring unit. The manuals give two causes: the flash memory does not have enough free space, or it has bad sectors or blocks. If the fault persists after the power cycle, you can also contact Huawei technical support instead of replacing the board yourself.
