---
id: mit:pre-installation-inspection
type: Mitigation
name: Pre-installation visual inspection
---
Pre-installation visual inspection means checking modules by eye before they are mounted. It is listed as a preventive measure against backsheet cracking. It is recommended together with continuous ground fault detection and a check that the IEC 61215 certification and bill of materials are valid. Backsheet cracking can start during production or installation, so checking modules before mounting can catch defects before they reach the field. Visual inspection is described as the quickest and most effective way to find module defects. It works well for burn marks, delamination, encapsulant yellowing, backsheet blistering and junction box failure. Look for bent or misaligned frames, surfaces or junction boxes, cracked cells and delamination, because these defects can lead to later power loss or safety failures. The IEC-based method splits the module into its parts and inspects each one under good lighting, counting only defects visible to the bare eye. Record the position and size of every defect and take photos without reflections. Its limit is that many performance changes cannot be seen and need more advanced tools to detect.
