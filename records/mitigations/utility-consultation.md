Utility consultation means contacting the local utility, the company that delivers electricity over the grid where the system connects. Contact the utility before you choose a grid standard on the inverter. The goal is to make sure the inverter runs with the grid standard the utility requires. If the inverter runs with the wrong grid standard, the utility may take action against the installation; the manual names cancellation as a possible result. The sources do not say which failure modes or fault codes this step fixes, and they do not say how well it works.
