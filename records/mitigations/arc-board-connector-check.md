name: Board connector check
---
A board connector check means confirming that the logic connector going to the arc fault detection board is properly seated and secure. It is the first step the inverter manual lists when the arc board error alarm appears, which points to an arc detection board failure. The same first step is listed for the arc protection alarm. Once the connector is confirmed secure, run the arc fault test from the inverter's Settings menu to see whether the alarm clears. If the alarm comes back, the connector check has not fixed it, and manufacturer service should be called to replace the arc board or the wiring box. Another inverter manual describes the arc board error as an internal self-check error or an abnormal reading inside the arc board, and says to contact customer service if the error does not clear.
