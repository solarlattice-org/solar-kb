An inverter settings review means checking the parameters configured in the inverter against what the site and the installation actually need. Before reviewing settings for this alarm, check that the meter is wired correctly and that its input and output wiring is not reversed. Parameters are usually viewed and changed through the manufacturer app, the LCD or the inverter settings menu. Wrong parameter settings can make the inverter misbehave, so only qualified personnel should change them. A wrong grid standard setting can cause improper operation, damage the inverter or stop it from running. Only use a grid configuration approved for the location. On some inverters, a saved setting does not take effect until there is enough DC voltage and the inverter restarts. Changes made in the evening may stay pending until the next start-up.
