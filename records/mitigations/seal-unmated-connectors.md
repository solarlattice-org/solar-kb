Close every unused or not-yet-mated DC connector or input with the sealing plug, cap or seal cover the manufacturer supplies. This applies to unused inverter DC inputs, to unused inputs on dual-input power optimizers, to unused connectors on microinverter trunk cables, and to optimizer connectors left open when the optimizers go in before the modules. The inverter is only properly sealed once all unused DC inputs are closed. Sand, dust or moisture getting in through open inputs can damage the unit and impair its function. Seal open optimizer connectors, especially if they are left overnight or in rain, snow, fog or dew. This simple step prevents an isolation fault, which is a loss of electrical insulation. The caps also protect unused connectors that may be needed later. Seal kits come with the equipment and can also be bought separately as spare parts. Sealing does not replace proper mating. Push connectors you do use fully home until they click and lock. Not every cover gives a watertight seal. One manual warns that its cover connector is not watertight and that the installer must add other waterproofing.
