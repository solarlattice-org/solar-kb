---
id: mit:spd-reseating
type: Mitigation
name: SPD module reseating
---
SPD module reseating means pulling the plug-in surge protection module out of its socket and putting it back in. It is a corrective step for an SPD warning on CPS string inverters. It applies when the inverter shows an SPD warning, such as the DC SPD or AC SPD warning, but the SPD status indicator is still green. Start by looking at the SPD indicator. A red indicator means the module is used up and must be replaced, not reseated. Reseating is used to investigate a suspected surge protector exhausted condition when the module itself still reads healthy. It mainly corrects a poor seating or contact in the socket. If the warning does not clear after reseating, contact the manufacturer's post-sales service. Reseating cannot restore a module that a significant surge or a lasting overvoltage has damaged. In that case the SPD has to be replaced. Many SPD faults come from improper installation, so check that the device is installed correctly before doing anything else.
