name: Cable strain relief
---
Cable strain relief is a corrective action for cable defects. It means fixing how cables are held and supported so they are not pulled or stressed at their connection points. It applies to damaged or undersized DC cables, which can lead to overheating, insulation faults, ground faults, lost production and fire risk. It is usually done together with replacing damaged cable runs, correcting cable routing, and shielding exposed wiring from UV light and mechanical damage. After the repair, check that the cable insulation is still intact. One installation manual shows a practical method: where needed, tie the conductors to a strain relief bar with cable ties. That example covers AC conductors in a battery system, not a PV array. The sources list strain relief only as part of a set of repair steps. They give no figures on how well it works by itself.
