---
id: mit:stabilized-encapsulant
type: Mitigation
name: Stabilized encapsulant formulation
---
A stabilized encapsulant formulation is an encapsulant whose additive package, such as a UV absorber and antioxidants, is designed to withstand photo-oxidative stress. It is a preventive measure against discoloration, meaning yellowing of the encapsulant. The encapsulant has to keep its optical, electrical and mechanical properties under UV and heat over the module's life, so low light absorbance is a key requirement. The additive formulation affects durability more than the type of base polymer resin does. Reduced yellowing has been reported for non-EVA polyolefin encapsulants, which also do not release acetic acid as they degrade. Additives can cause discoloration themselves. In damp heat testing, oxidised additives in a polyolefin encapsulant reacted with additives in a polyolefin backsheet and caused strong yellowing. Because of this, the encapsulant should be tested together with the adjacent backsheet and glass in accelerated ageing tests during product development, and compatible materials should be selected. The limit is that long-term outdoor experience with the newer polyolefin encapsulants is still lacking, and most durability data come from accelerated tests. Discoloration itself usually costs only several percent of module performance.
