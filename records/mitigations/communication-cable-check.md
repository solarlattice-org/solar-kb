id: mit:communication-cable-check
type: Mitigation
name: Communication cable check
---
Inspect the communication cable and its terminals for any abnormality and correct what you find so the connection is reliable. If the alarm is still active, reconnect the meter's communication cable. If these causes are ruled out and the alarm persists, the cable check has not solved it and Sungrow Customer Service should be contacted. RS485 communication cables should be shielded twisted pair. After connecting one, pull it gently to confirm it is secured and then tighten the swivel nut. Before working on cable connections, turn off and lock out the DC and AC disconnects and confirm there is no voltage in the wiring box. As routine maintenance every six months to a year, check that all cables are firmly in place and undamaged, paying special attention to sections that touch the metal enclosure.
