Cooling path cleaning means removing dirt and other obstructions from the inverter's filters, vents and heat dissipation path so that cooling air can flow freely. It addresses inverter overheating, where high temperature makes the inverter derate its output or switch off. First find what is causing the temperature derating and repair it, then clear the filters and the rest of the heat dissipation path. Check regularly that the air inlet, outlet or vent grate is not blocked, and clean it with a soft brush or a vacuum cleaner attachment when needed. On inverters with cooling fins on the back of the enclosure and air ducts on top, brush both clean with a soft brush after an overtemperature shutdown. How often you need to clean depends on the installation environment. Cleaning alone may not fix the problem: also make sure the inverter has enough ventilation, stays within its rated ambient temperature and is not in direct sun.
