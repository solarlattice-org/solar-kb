X-ray tomography, also called X-ray computed tomography or XCT, is an imaging method used to look inside a PV module. It can be used to examine less obvious delaminations at higher resolution than visual inspection gives, including delaminations that cannot be seen by eye. Delamination means the bond between the glass, encapsulant, active layers and back layers has failed, and moisture ingress and corrosion often follow it. Like ultrasonic scanning, X-ray tomography takes more time per examination than quicker methods such as visual inspection or thermography. It covers only a small physical area, so optical profilometry is better for measuring cracks across large areas of backsheet.
