name: Fuse used as load-break switch

This stressor is an installation error: a screw-type fuse is used to switch the inverter's AC connection on and off while current is flowing, even though it is not a load-break switch. Screw-type fuse elements such as DIAZED or NEOZED fuses cannot break a circuit under load. They are only suitable for protecting cables and must not be used as a switch-disconnector. Pulling a fuse while current flows can wear away its contacts. This can destroy the fuse or stop it working properly, and SMA warns that the inverter itself can be damaged. To disconnect the inverter under load, use a listed load-break switch or a circuit breaker. In systems with more than one inverter, protect each inverter with its own three-phase circuit breaker. This stops residual voltage from staying on its cable after you disconnect it. Do not connect other loads between the circuit breaker and the inverter. If loads must sit there, fuse them separately.
