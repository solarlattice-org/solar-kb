Other bits in the same register flag related conditions, such as the PID voltage stability alarm (Fault7010) and the bus over-voltage fault (Fault7000). Check which bit is set so you do not confuse them. The manual's first step is to watch the inverter for 5 minutes to see whether the warning clears by itself. If the warning stays on or comes back, the manual says to contact the manufacturer's after-sales service.
