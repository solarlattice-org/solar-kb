VDE is a German standards body that issues application rules for connecting generators and storage to the grid, including VDE-AR-N 4105 and VDE-AR-N 4110. Inverter manuals list VDE-AR-N 4105 as a selectable country grid code, and changing that setting is protected by SMA Grid Guard on some devices. Older inverters also offer grid settings based on VDE 0126 for Germany. VDE-AR-E 2510-50 is a strict German safety standard for stationary lithium battery storage in homes and small buildings. SMA battery inverter manuals have a section on the requirements of VDE Application Guide 2510-2.
