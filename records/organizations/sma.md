SMA Solar Technology AG is a manufacturer with its headquarters at Sonnenallee 1 in Niestetal, Germany. In the United States it operates as SMA America, LLC, listed at an address in Denver, Colorado, and SMA America documents shorten the name to SMA. The US service contact for SMA America is in Rocklin, California, and SMA Canada, Inc. in Toronto covers Canada. SMA France S.A.S. in Lyon is listed as a contact for medium power solutions. SMA's standard manufacturer's warranty applies worldwide, except for all of Latin America and the Asia-Pacific region. Australia and New Zealand are still covered. SMA sells a home system called SMA Smart Home, which it markets as a way to become more energy independent. SMA also sells business EV chargers. Customer organizations for these chargers are set up through SMA Service in the SMA eMobility Portal.
