Standards Australia is a standards body that publishes standards for Australia and New Zealand, some of them jointly with Standards New Zealand. Together with Standards New Zealand it issued AS/NZS 1170.2:2021, the wind actions part of the structural design actions series, which is used for wind loading on PV structures. Jointly published standards carry the AS/NZS designation. Inverter documentation points to AS/NZS 4777.2 for export limiting and for the DRM0, DRM1 and DRM5 demand response functions. Some inverters list an Australian grid standard setting shown on the display as AS 4777.
