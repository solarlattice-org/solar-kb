Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. is the manufacturer named in the user manuals and quick guides for its SUN2000 string inverters. The company is based at the Huawei Industrial Base in Bantian, Longgang, Shenzhen, in the People's Republic of China. Technicians can set up and troubleshoot the inverters with the SUN2000 app, which is available from the Huawei App Store, Apple Store and Google Play. More documents and technical support are available on the Huawei support website.
