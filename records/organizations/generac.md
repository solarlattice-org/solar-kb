Generac Power Systems, Inc. is an equipment manufacturer based in Waukesha, Wisconsin, and it publishes the installation and owner's manuals for its products. Its main solar product is the PWRcell Solar + Battery Storage System, and Generac provides a quick start guide for commissioning it. The PWRcell Inverter comes in single-phase and three-phase versions, and the installer chooses its operating mode on the inverter control panel. PWRcell system modes decide how connected REbus devices share power, and some modes use PWRcell Batteries to store energy or balance production against consumption. The PWRmanager has its own owner's and install manuals, which cover circuit groups and testing and troubleshooting. The PWRmicro installation manual covers location requirements and has a section on grid support functions.
