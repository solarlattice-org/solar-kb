DIN, the Deutsches Institut für Normung e.V., is the German standards body, and its standards are cited in PV equipment manuals and research reports. DIN 50018 sets out a test in which samples are exposed to a condensing, cycling humid atmosphere that contains sulphur dioxide. DIN also publishes EN ISO 6988, a sulfur dioxide test for metallic and other inorganic coatings under general condensation of moisture. An IEA PVPS review of PV module failures cites these DIN corrosion tests next to the IEC salt mist and ammonia corrosion standards for modules. SMA documentation for the WAGO I/O system lists DIN 60204 for the electrical equipment of machines. The same SMA documentation lists DIN EN 50178 for electronic equipment used in power installations, which replaces VDE 0160.
