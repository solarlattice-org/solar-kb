Solis is the inverter brand of Ginlong Technologies, which has its headquarters in Xiangshan County, Ningbo, Zhejiang Province, China. The company also has a US office in Dublin, Ohio, and publishes application notes for its US inverters, for example on how they handle the AC neutral. Its US grid-tied lineup includes transformerless grid support utility interactive PV inverters, such as the S5-GC75K-US to S5-GC100K-US models and the S6-GU EHV models. These are wall mounted and permanently connected. Solis also makes S6-EH hybrid inverters. In generator mode they synchronize with the generator and run grid-following, and some models have an RJ45 DRM port where the region requires it. SolisCloud brings in time-of-use electricity prices from Octopus, Nord Pool and Flatpeak for several European countries and uses them in its energy management.
