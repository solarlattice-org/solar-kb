NETA, the International Electrical Testing Association, publishes standards for electrical acceptance testing and maintenance testing. Its acceptance testing standard is called NETA-ATS and is used for new installations. Its maintenance testing standard is called NETA-MTS and covers insulated conductors and cable. When the manufacturer has not published data, NETA provides typical test voltages and minimum insulation resistance values for each equipment voltage rating. Encore Wire, a cable maker, says that under these NETA standards the acceptable field insulation value for new installations is typically 100 megohms. The NETA MTS and ATS standards, along with ICEA and IEEE 400, give recommended DC hipot test values for cable acceptance and maintenance testing.
