Schneider Electric is a manufacturer that publishes the Conext CL125 inverter documentation set, including an owner's guide, a quick install guide, an EasyConfig tool guide, a North American solution guide and a Modbus application note. The Conext CL125 solution guide lists the product part number PVSCL125A and is dated May 2018. In that solution, Schneider Electric also supplies the balance-of-system parts: Powerpact P and R frame AC breakers, a QED-2 series I-Line AC panel, an optional surge protection device and the LV-MV transformer. Modbus is a registered trademark of Schneider Electric, licensed through the Modbus Organization. For product information and technical support, technicians can contact their local Schneider Electric sales representative or use the solar.schneider-electric.com website.
