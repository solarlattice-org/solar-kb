CSA Group, often shortened to CSA, is the Canadian Standards Association, a standards and certification organization. CSA issues certificates of compliance for products, which then carry the CSA Mark. A C indicator next to the mark means Canada and a US indicator means the United States. CSA certifies products under its ISO/IEC 17065 accreditation with the Standards Council of Canada, and Solis inverter manuals include CSA certificates. Huawei SUN2000 inverter labels carry a CSA certification symbol for America and Canada, which shows the product meets CSA certification standards. CSA C22.2 No. 107.1 is used together with UL 1741 to certify that inverters meet the National Electrical Code and the Canadian Electrical Code. For some products, the same certification also covers IEEE 1547.
