name: DC injection limitation

- DC current injection
category: Protection
function_kind: protection
---
DC injection limitation keeps an inverter-based generator from pushing direct current into the AC grid above a small fraction of its rated output current. The Sungrow SG250HX-US datasheet lists DC current injection below 0.5% of rated current.
