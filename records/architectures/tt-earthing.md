A TT earthing system earths the power source directly. The consumer installation uses its own local earth electrode, separate from the utility earth. So the operating earth on the generator side and the earth on the load side are separate connections to ground. TT is common in home installations in many European countries. Some Solis models do not support IT grids or advise against TN-C, but every model listed supports TT. SMA backup configurations accept either a TN or a TT utility grid.
