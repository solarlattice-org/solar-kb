WREGIS is listed as one of the incentive-based programs that Locus Energy's incentive reporting team manages portfolios for. The source groups WREGIS with other utility and government-appointed programs such as PJM GATS, NEPOOL and M-RETS. For these programs, a revenue-grade production meter records generation data at set intervals, and the data is then formatted and combined for monthly submissions. The program validates the submitted data and issues SRECs to the account holder for the renewable energy system. Locus Energy's team handles the validation and submission requirements for each program and answers SREC-related questions.
