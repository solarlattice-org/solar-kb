The SMA Solar Academy is SMA's training program. It covers SMA products, systems, solutions and service, and courses are adapted to what participants need. Courses include webinars as well as in-person training at Academy sites in Europe, North America, Africa, Asia and Oceania. A global team of trainers teaches courses in the local language. The course list is on the SMA Solar Academy website. The Academy plans webinars and trainings on SMA Home Energy Solutions, and its instructors answer technical and general questions about the SMA Home Storage. For short learning videos on SMA products and systems, see the Tech Tip series on the SMA YouTube channel. The SMA knowledge article about the Academy is written for installers, who should log in with an installer account to see it.
