The California Net Billing Tariff, also called NEM 3.0, covers metering of electricity received from and delivered to a customer generator by the distribution provider. Rule 21 sets the interconnection, operating and metering requirements for generators connecting to utility distribution systems under CPUC jurisdiction. If a homeowner adds PV to an existing NEM 1.0 or NEM 2.0 system, California requires the whole site to move to NEM 3.0 under Rule 21. That change of tariff alters the solar credit the existing system earns, and it can leave the customer worse off depending on the size and type of the expansion. Enphase describes NEM-MT CT wiring as a California option for expanding net metering systems without changing the tariff of the existing system.
