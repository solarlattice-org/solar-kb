The SolarEdge Expert Installer program is a certification run by SolarEdge through its EDGE Academy online courses, aimed at deepening installers' technical skills. The residential course covers SolarEdge DC optimized technology, the home safety solution, DC safety, the top three installation mistakes, system handover, and working with the SolarEdge Service Center. Before starting, you must take the mandatory Fundamentals Certification Test. A separate commercial track covers commercial stringing, including a leapfrog wiring method for power optimizers, PV site mapping with the Mapper app, central commissioning of multi-inverter sites with SetApp, and installation best practices. Both tracks end with the SolarEdge Expert Installer Certification Test. In the North America training path, Expert training is the next step after the fundamentals and Home Hub courses, and it qualifies for 7 NABCEP CE credits. To take the course, enroll through EDGE Academy.
