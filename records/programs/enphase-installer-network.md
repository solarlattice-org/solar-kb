The Enphase Installer Network, also called EIN, is a group of installers that works with Enphase. Some EIN installers can now buy full design services from Enphase. These design services cover planned commercial projects that use IQ7+ Series or IQ8 Series microinverters. For a limited time, Enphase is also doing free design reviews for commercial systems. To ask about design services, installers should contact their Enphase regional sales manager. Enphase training covers what it takes to earn an installer certification and how to see installer certifications in the Enphase Installer Portal.
