The Sunny Highpower PEAK1 is an inverter product, also designated SHP 75-10. Its installation documentation is shared with the Sunny Tripower 60, designated STP 60-10, and is published in several languages. A separate operating-parameter description covers both the Sunny Tripower 60 and the Sunny Highpower PEAK1. Some SMA documents group it with the Sunny Tripower 60 and the Sunny Tripower Storage 60, and call all three simply the inverter.
