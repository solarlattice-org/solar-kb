The Sunny Tripower TLEE is a family of SMA three-phase string inverters. The sources name the STP 15000TLEE and STP 20000TLEE models. SMA sells a DC filter assembly as a spare part set for the 15000TLEE and 20000TLEE models, under the part designations NR-STP200-DCEMV and NR3-STP200-DCEMV. Known issue: in rare cases, Japanese-market STP-TLEE-JP-10 and -11 units show event 9011 and/or the message "connect AC and DC". For event 9011 there is no fix you can do in the field. Contact SMA Service and agree the next steps with them.
