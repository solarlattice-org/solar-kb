The Belden 7919A is a DataTuff Category 5e network cable made by Belden. It has four pairs of 24 AWG solid bare copper conductors with polyolefin insulation, an overall shield and a PVC jacket, and it carries an MSHA marking. The datasheet lists compliance with ANSI/TIA-568.2-D Category 5e and NEMA WC-63.1. It carries a CMX-Outdoor rating under CEC / C(UL), CPR Euroclass Eca and the UKCA mark. It is sold in black on reels of 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000 ft, and in blue on 1,000 ft reels. Bulk cable weight is listed as 30 lbs per 1000 ft.
