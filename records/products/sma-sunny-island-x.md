The Sunny Island X is an SMA inverter family. In SMA service documents it is shortened to Sunny Island, inverter. The family has two power classes, the Sunny Island X 30 and the Sunny Island X 50. The order codes are SI30-20 and SI50-20, plus SI30-LCD-20 and SI50-LCD-20 for the versions with a display. The spare part sets STX-INTFANSET-SP and STX-KP-SP fit all Sunny Island X models and also the Sunny Tripower Storage X 30 and 50.
