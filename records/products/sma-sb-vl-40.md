The Sunny Boy 1.5-2.5-1VL-40 is an SMA family of Sunny Boy PV inverters made up of three models: SB1.5-1VL-40, SB2.0-1VL-40 and SB2.5-1VL-40. SMA lists eManuals and downloadable manuals for the Sunny Boy 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 among its current Sunny Boy inverters. The SB 1.5 and 2.5-1VL-40 can connect to Sunny Portal through SMA Webconnect. Both Webconnect 1.0 and Webconnect 1.5 are supported, and Webconnect 1.5 needs the latest firmware. Webconnect 1.5 talks to Sunny Portal over TCP/IP. On company networks, you may need to allow outgoing TCP traffic to wco.sunnyportal.com on port 9524. SMA compatibility tables give different minimum firmware versions for this family, such as 3.10.xx.R and 2.03.01.R. The tables shown here do not say which SMA system or function these minimums apply to, so check the matching SMA document before commissioning. An SMA backup planning document lists firmware 1.01.07.R for the SB1.5 and SB2.5 models and 02.05.01.R for the SB2.0 model.
