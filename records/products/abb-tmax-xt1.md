Tmax is a family of ABB molded-case circuit breakers, which the SMA documents write as T-Max. SMA Sunny Central inverter documents list T-Max devices as the DC switchgear, and in one document also as the AC disconnection unit. For maintenance of this switchgear, those documents refer technicians to ABB document 1SDH000707R0001 at www.abb.com. The evidence gives no ratings, certifications or known issues that apply specifically to the XT1 frame.
