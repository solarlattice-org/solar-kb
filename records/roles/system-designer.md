On utility-scale battery projects, the site designer is the person responsible for all technical decisions on the project. This work covers civil, mechanical and electrical design, and the designer checks the site geotechnical report for geotechnical design requirements. The designer coordinates controls and operation modes as needed and consults the SCADA engineer on them. The designer prepares project-specific documents and drawings, such as the site layout, single-line diagram and network architecture, and coordinates and obtains the required permits. The Sandia inverter test protocol was written on the assumption that its results would mainly be used by a knowledgeable system designer. Designers often use design software such as SolarEdge Designer. The tool runs the design calculations, recommends inverters and power optimizers for the site size, and can export the array layout to the monitoring platform.
