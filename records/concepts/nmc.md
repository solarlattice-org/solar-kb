NMC stands for nickel manganese cobalt, a lithium battery chemistry. Some equipment documentation writes it as NiMnCo. Battery training material puts NMC next to lithium iron phosphate (LFP) as one of the main chemical makeups of lithium batteries used in solar storage. Storage safety reports list it alongside other lithium chemistries, such as nickel cobalt aluminum (NCA) and lithium manganese oxide (LMO). Watch the abbreviation: in IEEE generating-unit definitions, NMC means net maximum capacity, not the battery chemistry.
