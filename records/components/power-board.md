In Yaskawa-Solectria inverters, the power board is a printed circuit board assembly that supplies auxiliary power, and it can be replaced in the field. The replacement part is UTC001400, listed as an auxiliary power PCBA for the CR-BOX PC200-1500, and one board is supplied per replacement. The service procedure covers installing the replacement power supply board and then checking that the unit operates correctly. The manufacturer's customer-facing instructions include a procedure for replacing the power supply board, listed together with the midpoint filter board replacement. The service document uses a NOTICE label to warn of possible equipment damage, so follow those cautions while handling the board.
