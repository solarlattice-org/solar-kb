name: Open or blown fuse
aliases:
- blown fuse
- fuse blown
---
Take the fuse out and test it with a multimeter. A blown fuse reads open, with no continuity. Some inverters show a blown fuse as a fuse blown error on the device page of the user interface. Only check fuses on a de-energized unit. Turn the AC and DC switches off, wait for the inverter capacitors to discharge, and make sure there is no DC voltage or current before you open the fuse holders. A string wired with reversed polarity can blow the fuse when irradiance is high. The voltage across the blown fuse can reach twice Voc, which may stop the fuse from clearing properly and cause a fire. If all front-panel LEDs on a meter are dark, one possible cause is a blown power supply fuse. Connecting a test instrument to an energized circuit during a resistance test can blow the instrument's own fuse, so check that fuse before testing. Always replace a blown fuse with one of the same rating.
