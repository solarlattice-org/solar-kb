id: meas:clear-line-along-crack
type: Measurement
name: Clear line along cell crack
---
On a visual inspection, this signature looks like a lighter, clear line along a cell crack. The surrounding encapsulant has turned yellow or brown. Photobleaching works against encapsulant yellowing. It happens along cell cracks and along the borders of the cells. Small cell cracks usually can't be seen by eye, but the bleached line makes them visible. Encapsulant browning takes several years to develop, so expect this contrast on aged modules. Don't mistake it for snail tracks. Those are discolored silver paste on the cell gridlines, which also follows cracks. To confirm the crack underneath, use electroluminescence or UV fluorescence imaging. In an EL image, cracks show as dark lines. The failure modes behind this signature are cell cracks combined with encapsulant discoloration. Delamination along the same crack may also show up as small bubbles.
