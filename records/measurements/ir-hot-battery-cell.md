None of the evidence units describes a hot battery cell seen in an infrared image. The thermography material provided covers PV solar cells and modules only. The only battery-related unit lists battery temperature (BatTmp) as a display item on a battery inverter. It is a readout, not an IR signature. Do not read the PV hot cell findings, such as a uniform temperature rise across a reverse-biased monocrystalline cell, as findings about battery cells.
