---
id: meas:dc-bus-voltage-high
type: Measurement
name: High internal DC bus voltage
aliases:
- OV-BUS
---
This signature appears when the inverter detects excessive DC voltage on its internal bus and raises an OV-BUS alarm. To check it, measure the DC and AC voltages at the inverter and compare them with the values shown on the LCD. The manuals list internal damage and a wire that came loose during shipping as possible causes. If the fault is confirmed, the recommended action is to replace the inverter.
