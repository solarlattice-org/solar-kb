A fiber optic link is a physical transport that carries communication data as light over optical fibers rather than over copper wire. The Accuenergy AXM-WEB2-FOLC expansion module has one fiber optic LC interface and one Ethernet port, and it connects the meter to a fiber optic network. Serial links can be moved onto fiber with SEL accessories: the SEL-2829/2830/2831 transceivers send two-way serial data over two fibers, and the SEL-9220 turns an EIA-485 relay port into a point-to-point fiber port.
