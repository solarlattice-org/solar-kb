SMA lists KNX as a communication protocol, alongside EEBus, SEMP and Modbus. SMA defines a protocol as a standardized method for transmitting data. A communication standard is different: it is the physical transmission medium. So KNX works at the data-exchange level, not the physical level. Wi-Fi, ZigBee and radio are the communication standards that carry the data.
