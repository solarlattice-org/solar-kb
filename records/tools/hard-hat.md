A hard hat is head protection that guards the head against impacts and falling objects. It also helps protect the head from blows and from contact with energized electrical conductors. In higher arc-flash categories, wear the hard hat with an arc-rated or flame-resistant hard hat liner and an arc-rated face shield or arc flash suit hood. Battery storage install procedures list the hard hat as required PPE along with safety glasses, hearing protection, suitable gloves and leather footwear.
