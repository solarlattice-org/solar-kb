Solis Home and Solis Pro were the first generation of the Solis monitoring platform. End users used Solis Home to check how their plants were running. Installers and distributors used Solis Pro to manage a plant over its whole life and keep it running steadily. The apps included plant operation and maintenance management and remote monitoring of devices. To move to SolisCloud, log in to the SolisCloud app or the SolisCloud website using your old Solis Home or Solis Pro login details. A Solis Home account becomes an end-user account in SolisCloud, and a Solis Pro account becomes an installer account.
