Fronius Solar.start is a mobile app that makes the first commissioning of a Fronius Datamanager 2.0 much easier. You get it from the app store. Before first commissioning, either a Fronius Datamanager 2.0 plug-in card must be fitted in the inverter or a Fronius Datamanager Box 2.0 must be on the Fronius Solar Net circuit. To connect to the Datamanager 2.0, the laptop, tablet or other device must be set to obtain its IP address automatically (DHCP). To commission an inverter, open the app to connect to the inverter. Then run the commissioning wizard, which brings the inverter online, configures it and links it to Fronius Solar.web. After commissioning, open Settings and then Components in Fronius Solar.web to check for new inverter software. You can also start a firmware update from the inverter's web interface under System and then Update.
