PWRfleet is Generac's fleet management platform, and any REbus-enabled device can be monitored from it. Installers register a PWRcell system on PWRfleet. Get the serial number and registration code from the decal on the front of the inverter. Then open the PWRfleet website, or scan the decal's QR code and use the Register at PWRfleet link, and follow the prompts to complete the profile. When you register an inverter, every REbus component connected to it is registered too. The installation manuals for the PWRcell inverter, PWRmicro and the Smart Disconnect Switch all point to PWRfleet for product registration. To add a PWRmanager, the inverter must already be set up and the user account created in PWRfleet. Open the site, click Add Equipment and enter the unit's serial number and registration code. Then check that the PWRmanager icon appears on the site's devices page. PWRmanager registration is required for warranty coverage and for its load control data to appear in the PWRview app. If the system will not be connected to the Internet, the installer cannot use PWRfleet, the owner gets no PWRview access and the warranty is limited to five years. In that case, registration is completed by calling Generac at 1-888-ACTIVATE.
