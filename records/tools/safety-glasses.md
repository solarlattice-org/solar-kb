Safety glasses are basic eye protection and one of the standard PPE items that service staff at PV plants are expected to carry. Eye protection protects against dust and flying particles, corrosive gases, vapors and liquids. Safety glasses come with single or double lenses and can be made with prescription lenses, and they need to fit closely and comfortably. Goggles seal around the eyes, so they protect better against chemicals and vapors. A face shield is only a secondary device and must be worn over safety glasses for impact protection. Battery electrolyte can burn or irritate the eyes, so battery manuals call for goggles, rubber gloves, an apron and rubber boots when working on batteries. PV safety best practice is to wear safety glasses at all times, together with a nonconductive Class E hard hat and EH-rated footwear. Inverter and data logger manuals list safety goggles to protect the eyes from dust when drilling mounting holes.
