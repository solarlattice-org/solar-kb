Peak efficiency is a single percentage on an inverter datasheet. Some datasheets call it maximum efficiency and use the symbol ηmax. Datasheets usually list it next to a weighted figure, such as CEC weighted efficiency or European weighted efficiency. The weighted figure is the same or lower for the same model. For example, the SHP 150-US-20 lists a maximum efficiency of 99.1 % and a CEC weighted efficiency of 99.0 %. The Fronius Symo lists 97.30 % maximum and 96.50 % CEC. Battery inverters and microinverters also carry the figure. The Sunny Island models list 95.3 % to 95.8 % maximum efficiency. The Enphase M250 lists a typical peak of 96.5 at 208 VAC three phase. SMA names EN 50530 as the standard behind the peak efficiency of the SHP 75-10 and STP 60-10. These sources give only the rated values, not how the figure is calculated.
